Bespoke Sports, Wellness & Retreat Experiences

At Unique Locations, we believe that a memorable stay is about more than the destination. It is also about the experiences you share along the way.

In addition to renting one of the properties featured on Unique Locations, guests may request a bespoke programme of sports, wellness and leisure activities designed around their interests, objectives and preferred pace.

Our programmes are available for

  • Private individuals and couples
  • Families and groups of friends of all ages
  • Corporate groups and team events
  • Sports groups and associations
  • Private retreats and wellness stays

*Activities may be arranged as part of your stay or added separately to an existing booking.

Experiences tailored to you

Each programme is created individually and may range from a single session to a full-day experience, a weekend programme or a dedicated retreat week.

Depending on the location, season, group profile and availability, experiences may include:

  • Personal fitness and conditioning sessions
  • Running and endurance training
  • Guided hiking and trail experiences
  • Cycling and mountain biking excursions
  • Swimming and open-water activities (where available)
  • Winter sports activities (seasonal)
  • Yoga, stretching and mobility sessions
  • Functional strength and circuit training
  • Outdoor boot camps and group fitness
  • Team-building sports challenges
  • Children's and family sports activities, various ball games
  • Multi-day wellness and sports retreats
  • Tailor-made activity programmes combining sport, wellness and local experiences on demand

Whether you are looking for an active family holiday, a personalised fitness programme, a wellness retreat, a corporate team experience or simply the opportunity to try something new, we will work with you to create a programme suited to your needs.

*All activities are subject to availability, location, weather conditions and the individual requirements of participants.

Meet Kyle Graves

Bespoke sports and wellness programmes

Our bespoke sports and wellness programmes are coordinated by Kyle Graves, a former professional athlete with extensive international experience in the sports industry.

Originally from Canada, Kyle enjoyed a professional career in Canadian and European American football. He played professionally in the Canadian Football League and later continued his career in Europe, including with the Düsseldorf Panther in Germany and the Geneva Seahawks in Switzerland.

During his time with the Geneva Seahawks, Kyle played as quarterback and contributed his international experience to the development of the team. His sporting background also includes baseball with the Zurich Barracudas, as well as basketball in the Zurich region.

In addition to his playing career, Kyle has extensive experience coaching university teams in Canada and organising sports camps, training programmes, regular fitness coaching and active retreats for children and adults. His coaching philosophy focuses on creating engaging, personalised experiences that are suitable for participants of all ages and fitness levels.
